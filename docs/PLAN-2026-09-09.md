## Why (the evidence, so it need not be re-derived)

- 0/39 candidates pass the P&L gate on both selection and confirmation series
  (91,444 obs, 2026-09-08). Generation 1 is DEFINITIVELY refuted: MDE 0.4-0.6c,
  observed -0.5 to -2.7c/contract. Not inconclusive -- refuted.
- The search circles: 7/39 candidates read ONLY `implied_prob`; 12 read only
  midpoint + own price_history. A function of the market's own price can only
  win if that price is miscalibrated as a function of itself (favourite-
  longshot: 1-3c vs 2c cost). A third of all work is in a proven-empty space.
- The prompt steers it there: exemplar `favourite_longshot.py` IS a midpoint
  transform; tried-list sorted by skill (the artifact metric); "UNEXPLORED"
  names 2 fields; "abstain often" without a floor breeds unfalsifiable variants
  (gen 3: 6/8 the same idea, 16-644 fills).
- Learned facts DO reach the agent (CLAUDE.md loads from cwd; 11 candidates
  cite the cost hurdle, 4 carry SPREAD_TOL). What doesn't transfer: WHY each
  family fails. It gets a leaderboard, not mechanisms.
- Unexplored and well-populated: event coherence (`sibling_sum` used by 0/39;
  65.3% of obs have >=2 quoted siblings), book depth (3/39; present on 100%,
  median 92 contracts), time-to-close (2/39; median horizon 0.5h).
  `is_mve` is DEAD: 0 observations. Do not chase it.
- Replay ("backtest") here is a forward test executed by replay: INVARIANT #1
  scores only on outcomes unknown at created_at. Clean for book-only
  candidates. FATAL for research: research runs at scoring time and today's web
  knows last month's outcome. Research must be evaluated forward-only. That is
  MORE rigorous, not less; its only cost is time, and Kalshi's ~5,100
  resolutions/day make a selective candidate measurable in ~2 weeks.
- Structural insight: "evaluation is free so generate wide" confined the search
  to the free-to-evaluate space, which is the empty one. Research inverts the
  loop to few-and-deliberate. That is correct, not a compromise.
